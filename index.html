Gurukool · learning & teaching in the open

If you're curious enough to learn, just come and learn.

Gurukool is my attempt to follow curiosity — to learn and to teach out in the open, putting my actual way of working where anyone can see it. Because you learn a thing best by teaching it.

The name comes from a school my grandfather co-founded in pre-independence India, built on a single idea: the doors are open to anyone curious enough to walk through them.
